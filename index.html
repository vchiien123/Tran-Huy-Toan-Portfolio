TRAN HUY TOAN (CHIEN)

Multimedia Artist · 3D/VFX Artist
Motion Designer · Music Producer
Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

I’m a multimedia artist, 3D/VFX artist, motion designer, and music producer currently studying Digital Media at RMIT University Vietnam. I have a deep passion for creating expressive experiences through abstract visuals and sound. My work explores the space between CGI, visual effects, motion, and music, often through experimentation and unconventional ideas.

COLLISION CYPHER - Loài Trải Điên, Kaido, DanilV, TANZ, Acimous | OFFICIAL LYRIC VISUALIZER

'Unheard' - An Animated Short Film

2025

Unheard is a stop-motion animated short film exploring the struggles and oppression faced by women in a patriarchal society. The project was my first experience working with stop-motion, involving extensive experimentation with physical animation, illustration, compositing, and visual effects.

Credit: Screenwriter · Gaffer · Illustrator · Compositor · VFX Artist · Video Editor · Music Composer · Sound Designer

Special Thanks: Trang Pham · Thien Phuc · Quynh Huong · Nguyen Quy Nhat Ha

bedtime | a short experimental short film

2026

Bedtime is a short experimental film that visualizes the abstract and fragmented thoughts that emerge within the unconscious mind while falling asleep. The project explores experimental editing, compositing, visual effects, music composition, and sound design to create a unique and unexpected audiovisual experience.

Credit: Storyboard Artist · 3D Artist · VFX Artist · Compositor · Music Composer · Sound Designer

Belief | A Short 3D Animated Film

2026

Belief is a 3D animated short film exploring humanity’s almost religious belief in technology and artificial intelligence through a satirical lens. The project focuses heavily on 3D modelling, set design, and compositing to build a surreal world that reflects the relationship between humans and technology.

Credit: Storyboard Artist · 3D Artist · VFX Artist · Compositor

Special Thanks: Trang Pham · Quynh Huong · Thien Phuc

VinA/V #01 - DAM CHOI

2024

DAM CHOI is a VJ performance exploring Vietnam’s vibrant and often controversial entertainment culture. The project examines forms of fun and nightlife that are commonly shamed or judged, while highlighting their role in shaping Vietnam’s social fabric and nightlife identity. The performance heavily incorporates 3D visuals and live VJ performance to create an energetic audiovisual experience.

Credit: Director · Video Editor · 3D Artist · 2D Artist · Storyboard Artist · Animator · Music Producer · VJ Performer

Special Thanks: Quynh Huong · Mingool · Pham Van Khoa · David Le
